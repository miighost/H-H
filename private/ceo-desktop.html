Questions to Ask

Who makes the purchasing decision?
Do not leave without knowing the real decision-maker.
What products sell best in this category?
Learn before you pitch the whole range.
What would make a trial easy for you?
Remove friction and move to a concrete next step.
When should I follow up?
Always leave with a date, not “we'll call you”.

Common Objections

“We already stock dried fruit.”
Position Horn & Harvest around Somali origin, premium presentation and a low-risk trial rather than asking them to replace everything.
“Price is high.”
Move discussion from unit price to premium positioning, margin, uniqueness and customer appeal.
“Send information.”
Send it — but secure a specific follow-up date before leaving.
